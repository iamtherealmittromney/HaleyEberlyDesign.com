About

Strategy that starts with the people something is grown for.

My work sits at the intersection of care and cultivation. In healthcare marketing, I learned how to build trust through clarity — how to make complicated, high-stakes decisions feel human. In agriculture, I've spent the last two years translating that same instinct into a brand for a company that grows citrus, fruit trees, and plants: making the work of growing something real and something good, legible to the people who buy it.

I'm a strategist first — every identity system I build starts with a point of view, not a mood board. I'm looking for my next home with a team that's building something worth standing behind.
